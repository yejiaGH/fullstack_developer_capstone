About Us

Welcome to Best Cars dealership, home to the best cars in North America. We deal in selling domestic and imported cars at reasonable prices.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make buying a car simple, transparent and trustworthy. Every dealership in our network is reviewed by real customers, and every review is published unedited so that you can decide with confidence. We do not sell the loudest badge on the lot; we sell the car that fits your life, at a price that makes sense, backed by a dealer that answers the phone.

Our Values

  • Honesty first. We publish every customer review, including the critical ones, so the picture is complete.
  • Fair pricing. Domestic and imported cars at reasonable prices, with no haggling games and no surprise fees.
  • Local knowledge. Our dealerships serve communities across the country, and each one knows its market.
  • Service that lasts. We are still here long after the keys change hands.

Leadership Team

Leadership Team

Our leadership team oversees the whole network and keeps the review platform fair, useful and free for shoppers.

Our Story

Best Cars Dealership began with a single family run lot and a simple frustration: shoppers had no reliable way to compare dealers before they walked through the door. Today our nationwide network spans all fifty states, and this site gives every one of those locations a public voice. Whether you are buying your first car or your fifth, the reviews you read here come from people who bought exactly where you are about to buy.
